# 30-Second Data Quality Story (Alina / Rebecca)

Use only if conversation turns to KPI trust, pipelines, or exec reporting.

> "On a prior initiative I treated KPIs like a product: defined expectations on critical fields, tracked failures over time, and wrote downstream impact in plain language—e.g., if `customer_id` is null, we can't attribute revenue or CLV. That's the same mindset as dbt tests plus documented marts: one definition of approval or funded loans that PM, marketing, and finance all cite. I'd bring that rigor to Borrow so experiments and campaigns aren't debated on mismatched denominators."
