**Panel:** Explain complex data to non-technical stakeholders · lending/Borrow context.

## Non-technical explanation template

1. **Headline (10 sec):** One sentence conclusion.  
2. **So what (20 sec):** Dollars, members, or campaign impact.  
3. **How we know (30 sec):** Plain language, no jargon — "95% sure not random."  
4. **Next step (10 sec):** One recommendation.

**Avoid:** p-value, AUC, heteroskedasticity.  
**Use:** "range of likely outcomes," "held-out test group," "worst-case still acceptable."

## Example filled story (template — replace with your experience)

**Headline:** "We paused a dashboard metric launch because the number didn't match finance — and fixed the definition before exec review."

**Situation:** Marketing prepared a member campaign around "fastest growing product" using a BI tile that used submitted apps, not funded loans.

**Task:** As DS, flag misalignment before public messaging and exec readout.

**Action:** Partnered with PM and finance; traced metric to dbt model; documented denominator; proposed single mart field `funded_loan_count`; added not-null and reconciliation test (parallel to your data quality framework work).

**Result:** Campaign shifted to funded-member proof points; avoided reputational risk; KPI now one definition in Tableau and exec deck.
